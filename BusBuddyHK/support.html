Get help

Transit Buddy works without an account. If live information is unavailable, check your internet connection and try again. Search and previously saved information may remain available from data stored on your device.

For help, feedback or feature suggestions, email hello.busbuddy.hk@icloud.com.

For a technical problem, open Settings → Report a Bug in the app. You can review and remove fields from the privacy-safe diagnostic report before sending it.

Privacy Policy

Coverage varies by service. Some services provide published schedules rather than live arrivals. Transport information can change because of traffic, operations or incomplete provider data. Always check signs and announcements when travelling.
